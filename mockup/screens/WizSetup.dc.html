First-run setup

Welcome to Oculus

Oculus keeps your whole archive in one place: what came in, what it is, where it sits on the shelf, and what the public can see. Setup takes about ten minutes, and you can change every answer later in Settings.

You’ll set up your organization, how ID numbers look, the rights for your catalog descriptions, and which standard word lists to use. If you have an Omeka site, you can bring it in at the end.

Do you have an Omeka site?

Oculus can bring in an existing Omeka S site: items, item sets, files and links. Old Omeka addresses keep working because each one redirects to the new record.

How should ID numbers look?

Every record gets a catalog ID. Pick a pattern; existing numbers from an import are kept as “former” IDs.

Controlled vocabularies
